# Job Search Schedule — from July 2026

## Principles

The search may take months. Protect your health first. Two hours a day is the ceiling,
not the floor. Measure success by consistency, not response rate — responses are outside
your control, effort is not.

---

## Weekly Structure

### Monday – Wednesday: Research

- Search using the prioritised terms list
- Save links in one place as you go
- Write a one-paragraph summary per JD
- Shortlist the 15–20 strongest matches for the week

One task at a time. Close when the two hours are up, even mid-list.

### Thursday – Friday: Applications

- Work from your shortlist and research notes only — no new searching
- Submit targeted applications
- Target: 10 per week. Stop at 10.

---

## Ongoing (as needed, not daily)

- Respond to recruiters when relevant
- Follow up on applications that are more than a week old

These are not daily tasks. Do them when they arise, inside the two-hour window.

---

## Daily Rules

- **2 hours maximum.** Set a timer. Stop when it goes off.
- **One mode per session.** Research days are for research. Application days are for
applications. Do not mix them.
- **10 applications per week is enough.** More is not better.

---

## Everything Else

The job search does not get more than two hours a day. The rest of your time belongs to
learning, building, and staying well. A candidate who keeps growing during the search
is a stronger candidate at the end of it.
